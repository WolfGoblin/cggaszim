Service 02

Advanced Gas Piping Systems — PEX-AL-PEX

We install advanced gas pipelines for domestic and commercial properties using PEX-AL-PEX piping — a modern, layered pipe (inner PEX layer, aluminium seam-welded layer, adhesive layer and yellow outer PEX layer) that is a safer, longer-lasting alternative to copper piping.

Why choose PEX-AL-PEX over copper?

  • check_circle Cannot be stolen — contains no copper
  • check_circle No sleeves required in floors or walls
  • check_circle Fittings may be embedded directly in concrete
  • check_circle Rated working life of 50 years
  • check_circle Valves and manifolded piping available
  • check_circle Easy installation — no heat or welding required
